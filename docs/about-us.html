About us

PaxTech is a startup led by students from the Universidad Peruana de Ciencias Aplicadas, dedicated to the development of innovative technological solutions.

In a world where digitalization is key to success, PaxTech empowers beauty professionals with an intuitive and automated platform that enhances the experience for both them and their clients.

Through our solution, we aim to revolutionize the way stylists organize their schedules, reducing time loss and increasing their visibility in the market.
